Three moves, one quiet flow

Each step is designed so outsiders learn as little as possible.

  1. 1

    Shield

    Place ETH into a shielded balance, the starting point for every discreet transfer after it.

  2. 2

    Prove

    Each payment carries a ZK proof, evidence that the transfer is valid without spilling details. The payer's hardware computes it, so proving stays close to whoever pays.

  3. 3

    Move

    Send ETH onward, withdraw it, or run other onchain operations without needless exposure, while the outcome stays verifiable for anyone.

Plain ground rules

Nothing to sign

There are no wallet prompts, approvals or forms here. A page claiming otherwise isn't this one.

Numbers can trail

Third parties produce every figure. They can lag, disagree with other trackers or vanish for a while. Each gauge names its source for that reason.

Privacy has limits

Shielding narrows what outsiders can learn; nobody becomes invisible. Entry and exit points still touch public rails, and careless habits can leave patterns.

For orientation only. Nothing on this page is investment guidance. Young markets run thin and swing hard. Stake nothing whose loss would hurt.

Questions

Short answers about scope, sources and silence.

Heliport does what, exactly?

It gives ETH a shielded balance and a payment flow where sender and amount stay out of public view.

Where are proofs produced?

On the sender's own machine, per the project's design. The aim is a proving step that stays near the person paying rather than on a remote server.

Can anyone use it already?

Availability is whatever the register says. A wallet link appears only after it is stated; until then this page makes no promise.

Is this an official token page?

The strip shows a contract only after somebody states one. Until then, no part of this page stands for a tradable asset.

Why chase network statistics here?

Heliport resides within Robinhood Chain, so the panel shows that network's condition: value locked, newest block, gas price and busy markets.

Why does the block number move in jumps?

Blocks arrive far faster than a {{rpc_sec}}-second poll, so each refresh lands several blocks later; the counter then rolls forward to catch up.

Why does TVL differ from other trackers?

Trackers choose different apps to count and price assets at different moments. This page shows DefiLlama's figure exactly as published.

Does Heliport store anything about me?

There are no accounts, cookies or analytics. This tab calls the three data providers itself, so each of them sees your connection, as with any visit.

What do the icons at the top right do?

Both stay inert until their accounts are stated. Afterwards each turns into a link labelled by a handle read off the stored URL.

Can I pay here?

No. This page is informational; links lead outward to each provider's page, and any trading happens over there, under their terms.
